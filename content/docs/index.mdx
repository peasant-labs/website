# Peasant

Tend your harvests. Share your yields.

Peasant is the open-source CLI for AI coding transcript analytics. Ingest sessions from Claude Code, OpenCode, and more. Analyze metrics locally via a web dashboard or terminal UI. Share transcripts with the Village when you choose.

## What Is Peasant?

Every conversation you have with an AI coding assistant generates raw session logs. These logs contain a wealth of information — token usage, tool calls, model selections, conversation flow — but in their raw form they are opaque and scattered across your filesystem. Peasant discovers those files, runs them through a nine-stage ingestion pipeline, stores the results in a local SQLite database, and serves them back to you in a form that is actually useful.

Peasant is open-source, runs entirely on your machine, and sends nothing to external services unless you explicitly push transcripts to a Peasant village.

## Quick Start

```bash
$ peasant kickstart
$ peasant ingest
$ peasant tui
```

<Callout type="tip">
Peasant runs entirely on your machine. No data leaves your system unless you explicitly push to the Village.
</Callout>

## Features

- **Multi-provider ingestion** — Supports Claude Code (JSONL) and OpenCode (JSON) session formats, with schema reservations for Codex and Gemini.
- **Nine-stage pipeline** — A deterministic ingestion pipeline that discovers, diffs, filters, extracts, indexes, and computes metrics from your session data.
- **Web dashboard** — A local Next.js-powered dashboard served on port 8690 for visual exploration of sessions and metrics.
- **Terminal UI** — A TUI for navigating sessions and analytics without leaving the terminal.
- **Session tagging and metrics** — Tag sessions for organization and compute derived metrics across your usage history.
- **Village sync** — Push ingested transcripts to a Peasant village for sharing or backup, with visibility controls.
